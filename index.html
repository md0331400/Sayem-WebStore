Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download an app or game?
Tap the Download button on any app card, the featured banner or an app page. No login is needed — the button opens the publisher's own download link and the download count updates automatically.
Is Sayem WebStore free?
Yes. Browsing and downloading apps on Sayem WebStore is completely free.
Do I need an account?
No. You can browse and download every app and game as a guest. A free account is only needed to submit ratings and reviews, report problems, and use account-only features.
How do I rate or review an app?
Open the app details, tap the stars to select a rating, write your review, and press Submit Review. You can update your review anytime.
Is it safe to download from here?
We hand-pick every listing and link to the publisher's own download page, but we cannot scan or verify third-party files ourselves. Before installing, check the source, review the permissions the app requests, and read community reviews. See our Disclaimer for details.
I found a broken link or wrong information. What should I do?
Please use the Report Us option in the menu and tell us the app name and the issue — we fix it as soon as possible.

Privacy Policy

This policy describes exactly what Sayem WebStore collects, where it is stored, and what we do not collect. Last updated: September 2026.

Information We Collect

  • Account data (only if you sign up): name, email address, phone number, gender and password. Used solely to manage your account, your reviews and your reports.
  • User content: ratings, reviews and problem reports you choose to submit, shown publicly with your display name.
  • Signup attribution: when you create an account we store which source brought you here (for example a UTM campaign, a referring website, or "Direct") together with your own answer to "how did you hear about us". This is stored with your account record.
  • Anonymous aggregate traffic counts: once per browser session (not per page view) we add one count to daily totals for traffic source, campaign and landing page. These totals contain no identity, no IP address, no cookies and no device fingerprint, and cannot be traced back to you.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No IP addresses, no precise location, no advertising identifiers and no cross-site tracking.
  • No per-page-view tracking: aggregate counters receive at most one event per browser session.
  • We never sell or rent personal data to anyone.

Information Stored On Your Device

Your browser keeps: your login session, theme preference, a cached copy of the app catalogue for offline use, your pre-signup attribution state (deleted after 90 days if you never sign up), and a session flag that prevents duplicate aggregate counts. Clearing site data removes all of it.

Where Data Is Stored

Account data, reviews, reports and aggregate counters are stored in Firebase Realtime Database. Account passwords are currently stored in a legacy format while we migrate authentication to Firebase Authentication; until that migration is complete, please do not reuse a password from another service. See our security notes for details.

Third Parties

  • Downloads: download buttons lead to publishers' own websites or files, which have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.
  • Ad slots: promotional slots on this site may contain third-party ad code configured by the site operator; such third-party code is governed by its own policy.

Legacy Visit Records

Before September 2026 this site kept per-device visit records (including IP address and device details). That collection has been discontinued. Remaining legacy records are hidden from normal use, can be viewed only in masked form by the site administrator, and can be permanently purged on request.
